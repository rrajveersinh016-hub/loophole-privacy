FAQ

Quick answers about LoopHole on Android.

Is LoopHole free?

Yes. LoopHole is free to download on Google Play. The app is supported by ads. You can unlock short Pro periods by watching rewarded ads in-app.

Which platforms can I save from?

LoopHole is built for common social and video links (including YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, and more depending on availability). Platforms change often — if a specific link fails, try again later or contact support with the URL.

Where are my files saved?

Media is saved on your device into LoopHole’s library / local storage. We do not host your saved files on our servers.

What is Status Saver?

Status Saver is a LoopHole feature for keeping WhatsApp statuses before they expire (typically within 24 hours). It’s part of the same app — not a separate product.

A download failed — what should I try?

Check your connection, confirm the link is a specific post/reel (not a homepage or private profile), and retry. Some posts are private, deleted, or region-restricted. If it keeps failing, email us the exact link.

Do you collect my personal data?

LoopHole does not require an account. We may use device/ad identifiers and crash analytics via Google AdMob and Firebase. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is there an iOS version?

Not yet. LoopHole is Android-first on Google Play. iOS is on the roadmap — watch the changelog for updates.
